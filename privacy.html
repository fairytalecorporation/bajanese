Privacy

How the Bajanese website handles your information.

Last updated 25 September 2026

Who we are

Bajanese runs the Bajanese Restaurant in Lower Collymore Rock, St. Michael and the Bajanese Grill in St. George, Barbados. This page explains what happens to your information when you use bajanese.com.

What this website keeps on your device

Your order in progress, your favourites and your last 10 orders are saved in your browser's local storage so they're there when you come back. They stay on your device and aren't sent to us. You can remove them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Ordering through WhatsApp

When you press Send order on WhatsApp, your order and any name, pickup time or notes you add are placed in a WhatsApp message. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp. We use what you send to prepare and confirm your order. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to that message.

Forms

Our waitlist, catering and job application forms are run on Google Forms. We use what you submit only for the reason you sent it: letting you know when the app launches, quoting for your event, or considering your application. Google's privacy policy applies to those forms.

Services this website uses

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts provides the typefaces.

Like any website, these services receive your IP address and basic browser details when pages load.

No tracking or ads

We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies on this website.

Your choices

To ask what information you've sent us, or to have it deleted, contact us on Instagram or by email.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
