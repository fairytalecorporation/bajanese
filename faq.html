FAQs

Ordering, pickup, the Grill, catering and the app.

How do I order?

Add dishes from the menu to your order, then press Send order on WhatsApp. WhatsApp opens with your order written out. Press send, and we'll reply to confirm. Pay when you pick up.

When is the Grill open?

Thursday, Friday and Saturday, in St. George. Grill dishes can only be ordered on those days.

Can I order from both locations at once?

Each order goes to one location. Place a separate order for the other one.

Do you deliver?

Orders placed on this website are for pickup. Message us on Instagram to ask about delivery.

I have a food allergy. What should I do?

Tell us in the order notes, or ask our staff before you order, and we'll help you choose.

Do you do catering?

Yes, for parties, private events and the office. See catering & events.

Is there an app?

It's on the way, for the web, iPhone and Android. Join the waitlist to hear first.

How do rewards work?

Rewards launch with the app. Join the waitlist for details.

Where are my favourites and past orders saved?

On this device, in your browser. Clearing your browser data removes them. When the app launches, you'll be able to bring them into your account.
