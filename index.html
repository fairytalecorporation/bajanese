Our Story

The name says it all — Bajan and Chinese, together: Bajanese. We serve the full Chinese takeout menu alongside classic Bajan home cooking, made to order from the same kitchen.

Thursday through Saturday we fire up the grill for fresh grilled fish, grilled chicken, and macaroni pie — cooked to order, ready for pickup or delivery.
