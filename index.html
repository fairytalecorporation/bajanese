Our Story

[Placeholder — replace with your real story] Bajanese started as a backyard grill between family and friends and grew into a full catering service built around one idea: Barbadian food should taste like home, wherever you're eating it. Every plate leans on flame, citrus, and island spice — the way it's done back home.

We source what we can locally, marinate everything from scratch, and cook to order on the grill — no shortcuts. Whether it's a plate for one or a spread for two hundred, it comes off the same fire.
