The terms for ordering from Bajanese and using this website.

Last updated 25 September 2026

Menu and prices

Prices are in Barbados dollars (BBD). Prices shown on the website are estimates: our staff confirm the final price when they reply to your order. The menu, prices and availability can change.

Orders

An order is confirmed only when we reply to it. If something you ordered isn't available, we'll tell you and suggest an alternative or remove it. Grill dishes are available Thursday to Saturday only.

Payment and pickup

Orders placed on this website are for pickup, and you pay when you collect your order.

Allergies

Tell us about any food allergy when you order. We'll do our best to help, but we can't guarantee that any dish is free from a particular allergen.
